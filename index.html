"Life hard, but i go harder."

I'm a filmmaker and content creator based in Riga, Latvia, and founder of draftClub Studios — a production studio focused on short-form video for restaurants, local businesses, and brands building a presence online.

My work is shaped by years of travelling with a camera across Morocco, Ireland, France, Spain, and beyond — learning how light, composition, and timing make a story land. That same instinct goes into every client reel I shoot.
